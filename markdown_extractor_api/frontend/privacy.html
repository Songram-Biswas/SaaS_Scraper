Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your email address and securely hashed passwords when you register for an account. We also log the number of API requests made by your account for billing and rate-limiting purposes.

2. Payment Processing

We do not store your credit card details. All payments are processed securely through Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Please review Paddle's Privacy Policy for details on how your payment data is handled.

3. Data Extracted via API

We do not permanently store the HTML or Markdown data you extract using our API. Once the payload is successfully delivered to your application, the raw HTML and resulting Markdown are discarded from our servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@scrapemd.com.
