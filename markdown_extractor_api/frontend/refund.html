Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Merchant of Record

Because Paddle.com handles our payment processing as the Merchant of Record, all refunds are subject to Paddle's Buyer Terms and Conditions.

2. Subscription Refunds (14-Day Window)

In accordance with consumer protection laws (including EU regulations), you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial subscription purchase, provided you have not excessively consumed the API limits allocated to your tier. Refunds will not be issued where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse, or other manipulative behaviour.

3. Renewals & Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your billing dashboard. Cancellations apply to the next billing cycle. We do not offer prorated refunds for mid-month cancellations.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the 14-day window, please contact our Merchant of Record, Paddle, using the support link provided in your email receipt, or contact us at support@scrapemd.com.
